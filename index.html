Machine Learning & NLP

Online News Article Dataset Classification

Developed a robust text classification pipeline for the Online News Article Dataset. Implemented a custom NLP tokenizer with Snowball stemming and Chi-Square feature selection to handle high-dimensional text. Built a heterogeneous Soft Voting Ensemble combining an SGD Classifier, Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, and XGBoost, achieving a highly stable 0.7369 mean F1-score across 5-fold cross-validation.
